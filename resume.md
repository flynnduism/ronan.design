## My Philosophy

I believe in leading with empathy, adaptability, and a deep understanding of engineering workflows. By championing user-first design thinking in technical environments, I enable teams to deliver intuitive, high-impact products that scale with innovation.

- No 'I' in team — the best work is shared and built together, with no room for egos.
- Design for a "range" of deliverables. Projects will change — any design solution should consider a short- and long-term range of functional possibilities (be ready to adapt to API/data/feature state changes).
- Shape the process around the problem — meet collaborators at their door, in their language.
- Show, don't tell — move quickly with a 'can do' attitude.
- Share and communicate openly across the org. Show design thinking and share resources and progress across teams.
